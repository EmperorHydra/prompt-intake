Privacy Policy

AI Whisperer is a ChatGPT plugin. It does not independently collect or store user information outside ChatGPT and does not operate an external user-data collection server. No separate AI Whisperer account is required.

Requests and responses are processed within ChatGPT and subject to OpenAI's applicable privacy policy and settings. AI Whisperer does not control OpenAI's processing or retention practices.

For questions, contact us through GitHub Issues. This is a public forum; do not share sensitive information there.
